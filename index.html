🚀 SPACE STATION BOMBERMAN 🚀

Lives: 3 Bombs: 1

Controls

Player 1: Arrow Keys to move, Space to place bomb

R: Restart game

Game Rules:

  • Place bombs to destroy walls and eliminate enemies
  • Avoid getting caught in explosions
  • DANGER: Window breaches create vacuum! Stay away or run!
  • Distance 0-3: Instant death - pulled into space
  • Distance 4-10: Progressively weaker pull - keep moving away!
